Customer Segmentation & RFM Consumer Insights Analysis																
Business Problem											
The objective of this analysis is to identify distinct customer segments based on purchasing behavior and customer value, and translate those patterns into actionable retention, loyalty, and engagement strategies.										
Methodology												
The analysis uses transaction-level retail data and an RFM framework. Recency measures how recently customers purchased, Frequency measures the number of unique orders, and Monetary measures total customer revenue. Customers were scored from 1–5 across these dimensions and grouped into six actionable segments.					
Key Findings											
Champions account for 20.8% of customers but contribute 64.0% of total revenue, indicating strong revenue concentration among the highest-value customers.					
Needs Attention is the largest customer segment, with 1,525 customers, creating a significant opportunity for targeted engagement and reactivation.						
At Risk and Can't Lose Them, customers total 771, highlighting the importance of retention and re-engagement strategies.									
New/Promising customers account for 528 customers and represent an opportunity to build early loyalty and increase repeat purchasing.						
Business Recommendations										
Protect and reward Champions through loyalty programs, personalized offers, and cross-selling opportunities because they generate a disproportionate share of revenue.							
Develop targeted reactivation campaigns for Needs Attention customers using personalized product recommendations, incentives, and timely communications.							
Prioritize At Risk and Can't Lose Them, customers for retention campaigns designed to prevent further disengagement and recover valuable relationships.													
Nurture New/Promising customers with onboarding, personalized recommendations, and incentives that encourage a second purchase and longer-term loyalty.													
													
Source: UCI Online Retail Dataset | Transaction-level retail purchase data | Analysis performed in Microsoft Excel
